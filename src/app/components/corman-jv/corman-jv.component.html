Who We Are

A partnership rooted
in community.

CorMan Contractors (PNG) Limited is a Papua New Guinea-incorporated company operating since 2013. An incorporated joint venture between Mananda Umbrella Joint Venture Limited (MUJV) and Coral Seas Mining Services (PNG) Limited (CSMS).

Coral Seas Mining Services manages the company and maintains all licences for the storage, transportation and use of explosives, including licensed Shotfirers.
